Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about UrbanNest

How do I search for properties?

You can search for properties by entering a city or area and filtering results by price range and property type using our search tools.

Are property listings verified?

Yes, UrbanNest reviews listings to help ensure information is accurate and up to date before they appear on the platform.

How often are listings updated?

Listings are updated regularly to reflect availability, pricing changes, and new property additions.

Can I schedule property visits online?

Yes, you can request property visits directly through UrbanNest by selecting a property and submitting a viewing request.

Do I need an account to browse properties?

No, you can browse listings without an account. However, creating an account allows you to save favorites and track viewed properties.

Can I save properties to view later?

Yes, users with an account can bookmark properties and access them anytime from their saved listings.

Does UrbanNest charge users to browse properties?

No, browsing and searching for properties on UrbanNest is completely free.

Does UrbanNest help with price comparisons?

Yes, our platform helps users compare properties based on price, size, location, and features.
